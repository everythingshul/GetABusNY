How it works

From request to the ride

Send the route

Dates, times, every pickup and stop, and how many people. Choosing a vehicle is optional.

Get one quote

We check with operators and send an itemized price. Each pickup and stop is its own line.

Sign the agreement

Accept the quote on your phone and sign with your finger. You get a PDF copy.

Pay the deposit

Pay by card online. The balance is due before the trip and can be paid the same way.

Ride

Your driver has the route and your number. You get a text when the bus is on the way, if you want one.

About us

We find you a bus and see the trip through.

Get A Bus NY started in 2016. We are a charter bus broker, and we work with camps, shuls, yeshivos, schools and families. You tell us where the group is going and who needs to be picked up along the way. We line up the vehicle and the operator.

What a broker does

We don't pretend to own every bus. Some vehicles on our fleet page are ones we use regularly. For other dates and group sizes we ask the operators we know who has something free and what they would charge. That is how we can quote a 26-seat limo for one day and a 56-passenger coach for the next without telling you we're booked.

You also deal with one person. We write the quote, collect the deposit, confirm the operator, brief the driver and take your call if something changes the night before.

The trips we know best

  • Camp trips. Opening day, visiting day, trips and the ride home, usually with pickups in several neighborhoods and a tight schedule.
  • Shabbatons. Friday departures have a hard deadline, so we work backwards from candle lighting. Return trips leave after Shabbos is out.
  • Kehila trips. Shul and community outings where the route has a pickup at the shul and a few more along the way.
  • Kivrei Tzadikim. Day trips with several stops, long waits at each, and time for tefillah. We put the stops and wait times in the quote so the hours are not a surprise.

We also do yeshiva and school trips, Chol Hamoed outings, weddings and simchas, and group airport transfers.

How we work

  • The whole route is in the quote. Every extra pickup, stop and drop-off is listed. Our team prices each trip by hand; there is no automatic calculator.
  • Plans change. Flights get delayed, camp buses run late, a wedding runs long. Call us and we sort it out with the operator.
  • You can reach us. Our phone number is on every page. We are open Sunday through Friday afternoon and closed Shabbos.
  • Drivers have what they need. The driver gets the full itinerary and your contact details for the day of the trip, so nobody is calling around looking for the group.
